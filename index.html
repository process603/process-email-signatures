How to set it up

Set up both. The full signature goes on new emails only. Replies and forwards get the short one. That keeps long threads from filling up with logos.
Gmail
  1. Open Gmail on a computer. Click the gear, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature.
  3. Click Create new and name it Full. Come back here, click Copy full signature, then paste it in the box (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  4. Click Create new again and name it Reply. Come back here, click Copy reply signature, then paste it in the box.
  5. Under Signature defaults, set "For new emails use" to Full and "On reply/forward use" to Reply.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

Need the full card on a reply now and then? In the email you're writing, click the pen icon at the bottom and pick Full.

Outlook (new Outlook or Outlook on the web)
  1. Click the gear for Settings, then Accounts → Signatures.
  2. Click New signature and name it Full. Paste the full signature (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac) and save.
  3. Click New signature again and name it Reply. Paste the reply signature and save.
  4. Under "Select default signatures", set "For New Messages" to Full and "For Replies/Forwards" to Reply.
  5. Click Save.

If the logos or text come out bigger than the preview above, delete it, paste again, and choose Keep source formatting if Outlook asks. Don't drag the logos to resize them.

Outlook (classic desktop app)
  1. Go to File → Options → Mail → Signatures.
  2. Click New, name it Full, and paste the full signature (Ctrl+V). If a paste button appears, choose Keep source formatting.
  3. Click New again, name it Reply, and paste the reply signature.
  4. On the right, set "New messages" to Full and "Replies/forwards" to Reply.
  5. Click OK.
